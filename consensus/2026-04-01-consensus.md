## Final Recommendation
### Primary pick
**Voice-of-Customer Repository**

### Backup pick
**Creator Deal CRM**

## Why the team converged on VoC Repository
1. **Pain intensity**: customer signal exists but is fragmented across interviews, sales calls, support tickets, and notes.
2. **Market clarity**: early B2B teams clearly understand the problem and can describe current workarounds.
3. **MVP speed**: manual upload + AI tagging is enough for an initial pilot.
4. **Differentiation**: position as an execution/evidence layer, not another generic notes product.
5. **Pricing path**: team-level subscription is plausible earlier than broad B2C ideas.

## Agreed wedge
- Not: "store customer conversations"
- Yes: "turn recurring customer evidence into prioritized product and messaging decisions"
- Fresh nuance: add **now / next / later-safe roadmap communication** rather than expanding into a full roadmap system-of-record

## Key unresolved debates
- 얼마나 빨리 external integrations를 붙일지
- AI tagging 정확도 기대치를 어떻게 관리할지
- search/retrieval UX보다 decision workflow를 먼저 팔지 여부

## MVP boundary
### In
- transcript/text upload
- tag extraction
- recurring cluster summary
- evidence-to-action recommendation brief
- markdown/prompt export

### Out
- full CRM/helpdesk integrations
- complex permissions
- workflow automation hub
- enterprise governance

## Validation criteria
- 5~10개 팀 인터뷰
- 3개 이상 팀이 반복 업로드
- 인사이트가 실제 메시지/우선순위 변경으로 이어진 사례 확보
- 3개 이상 팀에서 `Notion + AI`보다 낫다는 반응
