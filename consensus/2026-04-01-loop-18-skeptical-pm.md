# Skeptical PM Memo — 2026-04-01 Loop 18

## Stance
- **Primary remains**: Voice-of-Customer Repository
- **Backup remains**: Creator Deal CRM
- **But** the newest evidence is not strong enough to justify broader positioning for either one.

## Read on the newest evidence
- On the VoC side, the fresh signal is useful because it makes `now / next / later` less hand-wavy by attaching operating horizons.
- But the same signal also exposes a limit: stakeholders still ask **"when is later?"** and bucket definitions alone do not solve the trust problem.
- On the creator side, the newest evidence keeps sharpening the pain around **collections operations, payment-system hygiene, AP routing, PO/reference memory, and clause completeness**.
- But much of the X/Threads input is still **indexed-snippet evidence**, not robust direct-source depth, so we should resist over-reading market breadth from it.

## 3 cautions

### 1) Do not confuse a clearer communication primitive with full category validation for VoC
The new roadmap-horizon evidence improves the primary's story, but it does **not** prove buyers want a large standalone roadmap-communication product. It mostly says teams need safer wording and clearer expectation framing inside an existing weekly decision ritual.

**PM caution**: if we keep adding roadmap surfaces, release-plan semantics, bucket settings, and status taxonomies, we may drift from the real wedge: **evidence -> prioritization/commitment brief**.

### 2) The backup may be misnamed and therefore look broader than the evidence supports
The latest creator evidence does not really validate a broad **"Creator Deal CRM"**. It validates a narrower **cash-ops / collections control** job: invoice readiness, payment terms, AP path, promised payment date, follow-up cadence, and blockage visibility.

**PM caution**: calling it CRM invites expansion into lead gen, relationship tracking, inbox sync, proposals, and contracts before the painful core is proven.

### 3) Evidence quality is still directionally useful, not decisive
Direct X/Threads access is still blocked, and the loop explicitly says Threads produced limited materially useful results. That means the newest social evidence should refine wording and scope, but **should not** be used as justification to add major workflow complexity.

**PM caution**: indexed snippets are enough to sharpen a wedge, not enough to license a multi-surface platform build.

## 1 keep
### Keep the current ranking and the narrowest useful promise
- **Keep VoC as primary**, but keep its promise tight: a **decision/commitment-safe brief** for founder-led B2B SaaS teams.
- **Keep Creator as backup**, but interpret it as a **collections visibility tool**, not a general operating system for creator businesses.

## 1 cut
### Cut the temptation to overbuild timeline semantics and adjacent workflow automation next
Specifically, do **not** overbuild:
- for **VoC**: full roadmap system behavior, elaborate bucket customization, public roadmap publishing, or heavy release-planning workflow
- for **Creator**: auto-reminder engines, contract/legal generation, accounting-suite behavior, or broad CRM/inbox sync

## What not to overbuild next
If forced to choose one thing to avoid next, it is this:

> **Do not overbuild the "when is later?" machinery into a full roadmap-management layer.**

Why:
- the newest evidence says bucket definitions help, but they still do not fully answer stakeholder timing pressure
- that means the product win is probably **safer explanation + explicit ambiguity + commitment hygiene**, not more timeline UI
- overbuilding here would pull the primary toward Productboard/Jira territory without proving that teams want to switch systems

## Skeptical PM bottom line
- **Primary challenge**: VoC is strongest when it stays a decision artifact with commitment-safe wording, not when it tries to become roadmap software.
- **Backup challenge**: Creator only stays attractive if we stop pretending it is a broad CRM and treat it as a cash-ops blockage tracker.
- **Next restraint**: build the smallest artifact that helps teams explain what is committed, what is not, and why — not a bigger planning system.
